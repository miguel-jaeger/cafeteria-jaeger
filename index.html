From the Andes to Miraflores

Our journey begins at 1,800 meters above sea level, where the thin air and volcanic soil of the Peruvian Andes create the perfect terroir for our signature beans.

We work directly with small-scale producers in Cusco and Cajamarca, ensuring every cherry is hand-picked at peak ripeness. Each batch is then transported to our Miraflores roastery, where we coax out the delicate notes of citrus, chocolate, and Andean flora.
